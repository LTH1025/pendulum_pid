WORK SUMMARY — A Personal MuJoCo Cart-Double-Pendulum Swing-Up Experiment

This folder records an assisted personal experiment in MuJoCo version 3.12.0 on the Ubuntu 24.04 platform (VMware Workstation in Windows), carried out with the help of an AI assistant. The physical system studied here is a "cart double pendulum" driven by a single horizontal force applied to the cart along a linear guide rail (coordinate "x"), unlike a torque-actuated acrobot.
The two-link chain hangs jointed at the cart and at the middle joint, and the only control input is the horizontal cart force.

1. The Goal of Myself

Starting from the double pendulum hanging straight down, drive it "up to the fully inverted (upright) position in one continuous motion", which without any full-circle rotations or flinging, and then try to hold it balanced upright. The cart is free to travel along "x";
No fixed set-point is required.

2. What was built and done (assistant-assisted)
  2.1 Simulation model (asset/pendulum.xml)
  Built and iterated on a MuJoCo XML scene(Start from version 2.2.1 to version 3.12.0): a cart constrained to a linear rail plus two revolute-jointed links, with the horizontal cart force as the single actuator.

  2.2 Controller design (control_pendulum.py)
  Implemented a PD controller ("PID-control learning"&"Ki = 0"), where the torque/force is computed in Python from the position and angular-velocity errors and applied to the cart.
  Structured the control as state-feedback (-KP·x − KD·vx − DP1·dq1 …) to pump energy during swing-up and to switch toward balancing. Reached a swing-up and capture pipeline that reliably raises the links to the upright region, with the best capture near perfect upright (small deviation and angular velocity).

  2.3 Simulation and Visualization
  Ran the closed-loop simulation in MuJoCo’s viewer and logged state/torque trajectories. Then confirmed the GUI/simulate fetch can only start from the VMware Workstation desktop, and the passive viewer is used for inspection.
  This folder’s "Animation for the output.gif" is the recorded animation of the run, and "the data output graph.png" is the plot of the recorded state/torque data.
  (generated by Matplotlib platform under the "fig/" output of the main repo).

3. Tooling and Environment notes
Coding by Python language, Simulator provided by Mujoco 3.12.0, Numpy, Matplotlib, Glfw in a Conda env "pendulum-pid".
(Files were transferred and run inside the guest through VMWare Tool commands and CRLF to LF)
(fixes were needed for multi-line bash scripts created on Windows).
